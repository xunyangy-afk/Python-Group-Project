## Refinements made after the first validation run

Refinements 1 and 2 were found on the full 8-company data, before the team fixed the scope.

1. **Placeholder news.** The first rule flagged 20 rows. Reading them showed 12 were long, real summaries that only said one of several articles was empty. The rule now requires the placeholder phrasing in a short text (< 1,000 characters) or a fragment (< 200 characters). Result: 8 rows on the full data, all confirmed by reading; 7 in scope.
2. **Look-ahead news.** A keyword search matched 33 rows, but nearly all were disclaimers stating that future information had been excluded, the opposite of a leak. The rule now compares explicit dates written in the text with the row date. Result: 1 row on the full data (ADBE 2025-02-15 mentions the 2025-02-25 Photoshop launch); 0 in scope.
3. **Encoded characters in filings.** Filings text stored characters as HTML entities (`&#8217;` for a right quote, `&#160;` for a non-breaking space, `&#38;` for &). They are decoded to the real characters, which changes encoding, not wording.
4. **Scope and timing after the vision was fixed.** The team chose seven stocks and a question about news and subsequent price and volume changes, so ADBE rows are excluded (D7), each news row and filing gets the first session after its date (D9), and prices get a 20-session volume baseline (D10).

## Conclusion: does the cleaned data still represent reality?

Yes, for the seven stocks in scope. Apart from the 828 ADBE rows removed by the scope decision, no rows were dropped and no values or wording were changed beyond whitespace and character encoding. Prices pass all logic checks, and the 7 large moves line up with known events (2025-04-09 tariff pause rally, NVDA DeepSeek drop on 2025-01-27). Every news row now points to the session that follows it, so the question about subsequent price and volume changes can be answered without look-ahead. Quality problems are visible through flags: 7 empty news rows, 1078 non-trading-day news rows mapped to the next session, 12 filings with no new risk text, and 28 filings that predate the price data.
